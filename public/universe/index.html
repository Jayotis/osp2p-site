OSP2P.com / Universe

UNIVERSE

See the network instead of hiding it behind a feed.

Universe is the signature OSP experience: a zoomable map for discovery, social context and understanding what your own node knows.

Start at your own star

Your place in Universe begins with the public social relationships and content your node legitimately knows. From there you can explore outward toward people, communities and public activity.

Zoom instead of render everything

A phone cannot draw millions of individual people at once. At far zoom, Universe shows aggregate regions and stable levels of detail. As you move closer, those regions resolve into smaller groups and eventually individual people and connections.

No false geography

OSP does not invent social relationships just to make a prettier map. Public evidence creates social geography. People without a public social line can still appear as isolated stars without being falsely grouped into a community.

Navigation is not surveillance

Panning, zooming and ordinary movement through Universe are designed not to trigger new social queries simply to fill the screen. The map is constrained by an explicit privacy allowlist on the node.

Explore, search and understand

Universe is not only a visualization. It is the Product Alpha's Explore surface, a place search can fly to, and a way to inspect how people and content connect without handing discovery to an opaque global engagement ranking system.

Product Alpha

Universe is being built before the outside tester release because it ties together cold-start discovery, the node's role, search and OSP's visual identity.
