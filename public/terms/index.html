TERMS OF USE

Terms of use

Version 1, 2026-11-17. The official OSP apps show these words the first time they start, and again once when they change.

  • Post only what you made, or have the right to share.
  • You answer for what you post. Posts are public, other people's nodes keep copies, and deleting a post reaches the nodes that cooperate, not copies someone saved.
  • Never permitted, with no tolerance in OSP's official apps and software: explicit sexual material; prolonged graphic or sadistic realistic violence; any sexual material involving minors; intimate images shared without the consent of the person in them; credible threats of violence against a person, and material made to incite it.
  • Anyone can report a post or person from the app. The project reviews every report, and official OSP software hides what breaks these rules.
  • Your node is yours. There's no account with the project, and the project can't change what's on your node.
