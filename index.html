FOR CREATORS

Build an audience. Not a dependency.

Your followers shouldn't disappear behind a platform's ranking system. OSP is being built around direct relationships, where people choose whom to follow and what to share.

01 / REACH

Your audience, without the gatekeeper.

Publish to the people who follow you. Discovery and delivery depend on the network and each person's choices—not on buying a boost from a central feed.

02 / SPONSORS

Your sponsors. Your deals.

Work directly with advertisers, negotiate your own terms and keep the revenue from deals you arrange. OSP isn't taking a platform cut of those direct agreements.

03 / SHARING

Let your supporters carry the story.

When people reshare public content, participating home nodes can help distribute verified copies. Your reach doesn't have to rely on one company's media servers.

Creator sponsorship tools are a future possibility, not a built-in marketplace today. Following someone does not guarantee that every post reaches every device.

YOUR NETWORK, STILL EXPLORING

Go live your life. Your home node can keep exploring.

Imagine waking up to new people, communities and public content your own computer found through the network while you were away.

OSP's home-node design lays the groundwork for background exploration: finding connections, locating available content and, when you opt in, helping carry public media you've chosen to share.

We want discovery to work for you, with controls over interests, activity and privacy—not a central company deciding what keeps you watching.

On the roadmap: personalized background discovery and overnight suggestions are a product direction, not a feature available in the current alpha.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF DISCOVERY

Algorithms should help you. Not control you.

Computers still need algorithms to find peers, verify information and locate content. What OSP is moving away from is a platform-controlled recommendation feed that decides who gets attention to maximize engagement.

People, trusted introductions and the connections you choose are at the heart of discovery.

THE SIGNATURE FEATURE

See the network instead of hiding it.

Universe is OSP's zoomable view of the social network. Start at your own star, explore public connections, discover people and content, and zoom from individual relationships out toward larger communities.

The map is built from information your own node already legitimately knows. Moving around it does not secretly query other people just to fill the screen.

At large scale, the node summarizes the sky into stable levels of detail instead of asking a phone to render millions of people at once.

HOW OSP WORKS

A familiar app on top of a different architecture.

1

Run a home node

Your computer owns the long-lived identity and stores your posts and media.

2

Pair your phone

The mobile app is the everyday interface for posting, following, replying and exploring.

3

Meet the network

Nodes discover other nodes, exchange signed public information and learn which peers are useful over time.

4

Share the load

Reshared public content can have verified copies served from more than one participating home.

PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE

The map does not get a free pass.

OSP separates people from machines and deliberately limits what the Universe may display. It does not expose IP addresses, relay addresses, private viewing history, hidden peer scores or guessed location.

Public discovery and public media still involve real network tradeoffs. OSP documents those tradeoffs rather than promising impossible anonymity.

CURRENT STATUS

Product Alpha is being built in public.

The protocol, home-node model, phone pairing, public posting, media delivery, reshares, replies, discovery and trust mechanisms already exist in working form. The current work is turning those mechanisms into a product people will actually want to use — profiles, Universe, discovery, visible network activity and a polished first-run experience.
